About this work

I am Sindhuja Gouda, a student on MG3003 Enterprise HRIS Architecture at Silicon University. The course ran as a simulation: we played consultants for GlobalTech, a fictional technology company, and made real design decisions about its HR systems, week by week.

Weeks 1-3 were presented as part of Team 1 (Anikesh Ransingh, Gayatri Pati, Swastik Ranjan Das, Ritika Behera, Abhisekh Mohanty and me). Weeks 4-5 were assessed in the mid-semester exam. Weeks 6-10 were end-term sprints, each with three deliverables.

MG3003 · Silicon University · Instructor: Subhankar Pattanayak · GlobalTech Inc. is a fictional case company; all figures belong to the case.
